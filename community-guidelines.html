Community Guidelines & AI Content Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Pocket Twin lets you create AI avatars and generate images, videos, and conversations powered by artificial intelligence. To keep the experience safe and welcoming for everyone, all users must follow these guidelines whenever they create or share content in the App.

Every image, video, and chat response in Pocket Twin is generated by AI. Content may be inaccurate, unrealistic, or unintentionally produce unexpected results. Use your own judgment and do not treat AI output as factual or as the words of any real person.

1. What is not allowed

You must not use Pocket Twin to create, request, or share content that:

  • Sexualises minors in any form. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and report it to the relevant authorities.
  • Contains sexually explicit, pornographic, or otherwise obscene material.
  • Depicts non-consensual sexual content or sexual violence.
  • Promotes, glorifies, or provides instructions for self-harm, suicide, or eating disorders.
  • Depicts graphic violence, gore, or torture.
  • Promotes terrorism, violent extremism, or harm against any person or group.
  • Contains hate speech or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic.
  • Harasses, bullies, defames, or threatens any individual.
  • Provides instructions for creating weapons, explosives, or illegal drugs.
  • Impersonates real people (including public figures) in defamatory, sexual, or misleading scenarios — i.e. deepfakes intended to deceive or harm.
  • Infringes intellectual property, privacy, or publicity rights of others.
  • Attempts to bypass our safety filters or to extract our system prompts.

2. Use of your own likeness and others

  • You may only upload photos of yourself, or photos of others for which you have explicit consent.
  • Do not create avatars or AI content featuring private individuals without their knowledge and permission.
  • Do not use AI-generated content to deceive, defraud, or impersonate.

3. AI-generated content disclosure

Content created in Pocket Twin (chat replies, images, videos) is generated by AI and labelled accordingly inside the App. If you share AI-generated content outside the App, please disclose that it is AI-generated, especially when it could be mistaken for a real person, event, or statement.

4. How to report content

If you see content in the App that violates these guidelines:

  • Tap the flag icon next to any AI message in chat.
  • Tap the Report button on any generated image or video, or the flag icon in the full-screen viewer.
  • Use Settings → Report Content or Issue for anything else.
  • Email sales@atech-tools.com with details and screenshots if needed.

Every report is reviewed by our team. We use reports to improve our content filters, block repeat offenders, and remove harmful content.

5. Enforcement

We may take any of the following actions when these guidelines are violated:

  • Remove the offending content.
  • Issue a warning to the account.
  • Restrict or disable features for the account (e.g. generation, chat).
  • Suspend or permanently terminate the account.
  • Report illegal content to law enforcement and applicable authorities (including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children for CSAM).

Important: Content involving the sexualisation of minors will result in immediate, permanent account termination and reporting to law enforcement, without warning.

6. Our safeguards

  • Generation prompts are screened by an automated safety filter before being sent for processing.
  • Our AI conversation model is configured to refuse disallowed content, even when asked indirectly.
  • Upstream AI providers (OpenAI, fal.ai, ElevenLabs) apply additional moderation and may refuse requests that violate their policies.
  • Generated content is labelled as AI-generated wherever it appears in the App.
  • Every piece of AI content can be reported in one tap.

7. Contact

For policy questions, appeals, or to report harmful content, contact us at sales@atech-tools.com.
